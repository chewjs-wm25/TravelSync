* **主色与副色 (Brand Colors)**

* **副色 (Secondary):** `Ocean #4ECDC4` (`secondary-500`)
* **点缀色 (Accent):** `Sun #FFE66D` (`accent-400`)


* **中性色 (Neutrals)**
* **页面背景 (Background):** `Light Gray #F3F4F6` (`gray-100`)
* **便当盒卡片 (Card/Surface):** `White #FFFFFF`
* **主标题/正文重 (Text High):** `Dark #1F2937` (`gray-800`)
* **正文/次级文本 (Text Muted):** `Slate #6B7280` (`gray-500`)

## 3. 间距与网格系统 (Spacing & Grid)

* **基准步进:** `4px`
* **便当盒间距 (Bento Gaps):**
* 移动端: **16px (`gap-4`)**
* 桌面端: **24px (`gap-6`)**


* **内边距规范 (Paddings):**
* 常规便当卡片 (Standard Module): `p-6` (24px)
* 大面积便当卡片 (Hero Module): `p-8` (32px)
* 小组件/操作条 (Micro Module): `p-4` (16px)
* 按钮 (Buttons): `px-6 py-3`



---

## 4. 圆角与阴影 (Radius & Elevation)

* **圆角规范 (Border Radius):**
* 主便当盒卡片 (Main Bento Cards): **24px** (`rounded-3xl`)
* 内部小卡片/图库 (Inner Images): **16px** (`rounded-2xl`)
* 主操作按钮 (Primary CTA): **9999px** (`rounded-full`)
* 小标签/徽章 (Tags/Badges): **8px** (`rounded-md`)


* **投影与层级 (Shadows):**
* Base (无交互): `shadow-[0_2px_20px_rgba(0,0,0,0.03)]`
* Hover/Active (悬浮): `shadow-[0_12px_32px_rgba(255,107,107,0.15)]`
* Modal/Popover (模态框): `shadow-2xl`

## 6. 布局与响应式规则 (Layout & Breakpoints)

* **断点定义 (Breakpoints):**
* **Mobile (`< 768px`):** `grid-cols-1`
* **Tablet (`md: >= 768px`):** `grid-cols-2`
* **Desktop (`lg: >= 1024px`):** `grid-cols-4`
* **Wide (`xl: >= 1280px`):** 跨列组合 (`col-span-2 row-span-2`)


* **版心与栅格规范:**
* **最大宽度:** **1280px** (`max-w-7xl mx-auto`)
* **两侧安全边距:** 移动端 `px-4` (16px)，平板 `px-6` (24px)，桌面端 `px-8` (32px)



---

## 7. 交互与微动画 (Interactions & Motion)

* **过渡时间 (Duration):**
* 微交互: **150ms** (`duration-150`)
* 大组件变化: **300ms** (`duration-300`)


* **缓动曲线 (Easing):**
* 弹性弹出: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` (`ease-bento`)
* 常规 Hover: `ease-out`
* 悬浮效果: `-translate-y-1`


* **Toast 反馈机制:**
* 弹出位置: `top-5 left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2`
* 动画表现: `slide-in-from-top-4 fade-in`
* 停留时间: **3000ms**
